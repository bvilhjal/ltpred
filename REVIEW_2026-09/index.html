    Independent review of ltpred v0.4.2 (2026-08-28)¶

    A read-only, peer-review-style audit of the statistical core, the estimation and fitting APIs, the supporting numerics, the test and CI machinery, the benchmark evidence base, and the documentation. This review covers the tree at commit 7806e55 (v0.4.2 plus its unreleased changelog entries); it follows, and is independent of, the v0.3.4 audit in REVIEW_2026-08.md. The tier-3 findings it reports were fixed in the same change set that adds this document (section 4 lists the dispositions; see the CHANGELOG for the release entries).

    1. Scope and method¶

    The review proceeded in three stages. First, the project's own gates were run: the full test suite, the linter, the benchmark-evidence guard, and the example scripts (Table 1). Second, the statistical core was audited against primary sources and closed forms: the liability-threshold covariance assembly, the Pearson-Aitken selection update and the PA-FGRS censored-control mixture (Dybdahl Krebs et al. 2024, supp. eqs. S3-S5), the truncated-MVN Gibbs conditionals (Kotecha & Djuric 1999), the Kaplan-Meier and Aalen-Johansen cumulative-incidence estimators (Andersen, Borgan, Gill & Keiding 1993; the cmprsk::cuminc finite-risk-set variance), the tetrachoric MLE, the Haseman-Elston variance-component fixed point, and the threshold/CIP conversions. Third, every headline claim was re-verified independently: the numerics in section 3 were recomputed from closed forms, textbook examples, or simulation ground truth rather than taken from the package's own tests. Severity tiers: T1, correctness or scientific validity; T2, robustness, evidence integrity, or maintainability; T3, style, clarity, or polish.

    2. Verdicts by area¶

    Statistical core (covariance.py, thresholds.py, pearson_aitken.py, gibbs.py). Correct. The role-grammar shared-DNA fractions (parents, sibs, grandparents, half-sibs, avuncular, children across partner groups) were checked pair by pair; the tabular-method kinship computer (Henderson 1976) was checked on hand-built pedigrees including a first-cousin pair and a sib-mating inbreeding loop (A[i,i] = 1.25); the positive-definite nudge was checked on singular and indefinite matrices. The bound conventions (case above threshold, onset pins, censored controls), the -ppf(p) tail-safe quantiles, the log-CDF far-tail expansions, and the far-right-tail truncated-normal sampler and moments were all verified against high-precision references. The PA sequential update is exactly the classical rank-1 selection update, and its active-leading-block optimisation was verified algebraically (coordinates already folded never re-enter the target's moments).

    Estimation (estimate.py). Correct. The Pearson-Aitken fold order is canonicalised (sorted roles), so results are a property of the family structure, not of input row order; Gibbs keeps caller order because the sweep consumes RNG per coordinate, and per-family in-kernel seeding makes seeded results independent of prange scheduling. The collapsed (Rao-Blackwellised) genetic rows agree with the full chain and with closed forms (section 3). The batch-means Monte-Carlo SE (R batchmeans::bmmat convention) and the posterior variance are kept as two distinct quantities, and both are reported with the right caveats. The multi-trait covariance assembler was verified block by block against hand computation, including the same-individual cross-trait entries and the PSD coherence checks on full_corrmat - D genetic_corrmat D.

    Fitting (fit.py). Sound, with a deliberately defensive sampling contract. The pooled Haseman-Elston fixed point rejects personalised or onset-pinned bounds (outside its common-threshold estimating contract), tests each role's observed case rate against the prevalence the thresholds assert (binomial z; Kish effective n under IPW), rejects pid-overlapping families, and warns on unascertained use without an explicit sampling=. The ascertainment guard was verified to fire on a case-enriched cohort, where the fitter would otherwise silently pin h2 at 1.0. The h2_se output is honestly described as a within-dataset Monte-Carlo diagnostic, not an inferential SE.

    CIP estimation (cip.py). Correct. The risk-set convention (entry < t <= exit), the product-limit and Aalen-Johansen increments, and the Greenwood and finite-risk-set tie-correct Aalen (1978) variances were checked against textbook hand-computed examples and against an exponential competing-risks ground truth (section 3). Zero-length follow-ups carrying an event are rejected rather than silently corrupting a risk set.

    Diagnostics and conversions (tetrachoric.py, liability_scale.py). Correct. The tetrachoric MLE recovers a known latent correlation on simulated data; the Lee et al. (2011) observed-to-liability factors and the Lee-Wray null subtraction are stated with the right unit caveats (residual probit scale vs fraction of total liability).

    Simulation (simulate.py). Coherent. The age-aware generator's threshold-crossing onset model is exactly invertible against the threshold helpers, and the onset-resolution (register grid) handling converts point pins into the correct two-sided liability intervals. Generation gaps keep parents older than their probands.

    Tests, CI, benchmarks, docs. Strong. The suite pins hand-computed oracles (hard-coded Aalen variances, textbook Kaplan-Meier values), and the CI matrix covers Python 3.9-3.14t plus macOS, the no-Numba pure-Python fallback, the declared dependency floors, every example script, and a wheel install away from the source tree. Benchmarks are provenance-marked historical snapshots whose claims a CI guard recomputes from the committed CSVs. The docs and the README distinguish what each comparison does and does not validate (in particular, that the PA-Gibbs agreement checks do not validate the PA-only censoring mixture).

    4. Findings and dispositions¶

    No T1 or T2 findings.

    • T3-1 tetrachoric_table accepted fractional cell counts silently (e.g. 10.5 cases). Fixed: counts are now required to be nonnegative integers; integral floats such as 5.0 remain accepted, with a regression test.
    • T3-2 correct_positive_definite performed correction_limit + 1 corrections because the loop bound was n <= correction_limit, disagreeing with its documented contract. Fixed: the bound is now n < correction_limit, so the limit is the number of attempts; a limit of 0 rejects an unrepaired matrix, with a regression test.
    • T3-3 Seeding rtmvnorm_gibbs mutates the process-global NumPy RNG, so two concurrent seeded calls to the low-level sampler can interfere. The high-level estimators are unaffected (per-family in-kernel seeding). Documented on the seed parameter; no code change needed.
    • T3-4 The per-family Gibbs seed blocks wrap modulo 2^32, so n * max_rounds > 2^32 (beyond ~43M families per call at the default max_rounds) could collide two families' streams. Documented in _base_seeds; no code change needed.
    • T3-5 _AGE_RANGES / _age_range in ltpred.simulate were dead code kept only because a test pinned the role-stem parsing bug through them. Fixed: removed from the package; the regression pin now lives in the test suite and exercises _role_stem, which _draw_ages actually uses.

    5. Second pass: the review branch itself¶

    The branch carrying the section-4 dispositions (review/2026-09, commits 6885132 and 2000857) was then reviewed with the same method, and the two declared gaps of the first pass were closed: the documentation site was built locally under mkdocs build --strict in a fresh venv (clean; the new review page renders in the nav with its tables intact), the evidence guard was run in that same venv (mirroring the CI docs job), and the remaining two example scripts were run end to end (ltfh_power_demo.py: PA–Gibbs agreement 0.9997; registry_pipeline.py: 0.9999). The equations of docs/algorithm.md were checked line by line against the code (the precision-form Gibbs conditionals, the inverse-CDF sweep, the PA rank-1 update, the collapse variance identity, and the mixture weight all match). Every exported name was confirmed to render in the built API page via the per-module mkdocstrings directives.

    Two findings. T3-6 (a defect in the first pass's own T3-1 fix): the fractional-count gate ran math.floor before any finiteness check, so an infinite count raised a bare OverflowError instead of a ValueError (on main such input was caught only incidentally, downstream, by the monomorphism check). Fixed: a finiteness gate now precedes the integer gate; nan and inf counts raise a clean ValueError, with a regression test. T3-7 (observation, no action): the estimator's collapsed-kernel Gibbs reports a Monte-Carlo SE about an order of magnitude smaller than offline batch_means on raw rtmvnorm_gibbs draws for the same draw count, because the collapsed kernel streams the Rao-Blackwellised conditional mean rather than raw g draws. This is by design and is documented in docs/algorithm.md step G2; it is recorded here so a cross-check against the low-level sampler is not misread as a bug.

    6. What this review did not do¶

    The R-package lock comparisons (LTFHPlus 2.2.0, LTFGRS 1.0.1) and the HAPNEST real-LD benchmark path were not re-run (they need R and external genotype data); their stored artifacts were checked for internal consistency by scripts/check_evidence.py instead. The full benchmark suite was not re-run (the provenance note in benchmarks/RESULTS.md already records the snapshot procedure). The documentation site was rebuilt locally in the second pass, as recorded in section 5; it was not deployed from the review branch.
